# Patch notes

## 2.0.0 — The Village Square Update

Talking to villagers now changes the game: ask a villager for work and it gives you a real request (bring items or
hunt monsters), pays you in emeralds and XP, adds vanilla positive gossip about you, and tells its neighbours.
Replies float above villagers in speech bubbles. Villagers know your standing (stranger, friend, hero, or someone they
don't trust) and refuse work if you hurt them. Three advancements. Works with any OpenAI-compatible AI provider, a
local model, or no AI at all. Available for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge), 1.21.1 (Fabric, Forge, NeoForge),
26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge). 1.x worlds and configs carry over.

Full notes: [patchnotes/2.0.0.md](patchnotes/2.0.0.md).
